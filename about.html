About Nortava

Zimbabwe's nationwide direct-to-landlord real estate platform

Our Mission

Nortava was born in Norton with a simple mission—eliminate scam listings and endless agent delays—and now serves property seekers and verified landlords across all 10 provinces of Zimbabwe. We bring real, document-screened listings together in one transparent platform.

Why Nortava Across Zimbabwe?

Every city, town, and growth point in Zimbabwe has distinct suburbs, zones, and rental norms. We map locations all the way down to suburbs and estates (from Knowe, Galloway, and Katanga in Norton to Borrowdale in Harare, Suburbs in Bulawayo, and across every province) while requiring Title Deeds / Cession papers and Zimbabwe National IDs from landlords to stop con artists.

Regulatory Transparency (Zimbabwe)

Nortava operates strictly as an online property listing directory—not as a licensed estate agent under the Estate Agents Act [Chapter 27:17]. We never collect rent, security deposits, or sales commissions, and we handle personal information in accordance with Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]. Review our Terms, Privacy Policy, and Zimbabwe Tenancy Safety Guide.
