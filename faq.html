General Questions

What is Nortava?
Nortava is a Zimbabwe-wide direct real estate platform covering all 10 provinces—including Harare, Bulawayo, Norton, Mutare, Gweru, Masvingo, Chinhoyi, Victoria Falls, and more. We connect tenants and buyers with document-verified landlords.
Is Nortava free to use?
Browsing listings and asking Tariro AI to match homes by area and conditions is completely free. Verified landlords can list properties for free. Tenants only pay a small $2.50 fee when they want to unlock a landlord's contact details.
Which parts of Zimbabwe does Nortava cover?
Nortava covers all 10 Provinces of Zimbabwe with structured hierarchy down to Cities/Towns, Zones, and actual Suburbs & Estates (e.g., Knowe, Galloway, and Katanga under Norton in Mashonaland West, plus Borrowdale, Avondale, Suburbs, Morningside, Chikanga, Mkoba, and dozens more nationwide).

For Tenants

How do I contact a landlord?
When you find a property you like, click "Unlock Contact" and pay the $2.50 fee using your preferred mobile payment method. You'll immediately get access to the landlord's phone number and can reach them via call or WhatsApp.
Is the unlock fee a one-time payment?
Yes! Once you pay to unlock a landlord's contact, you have permanent access. The contact details are saved in your dashboard forever.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept EcoCash, OneMoney, Mukuru, and InnBucks - all popular mobile payment methods in Zimbabwe.

For Landlords

How do I list my property?
Create a free landlord account, go to your dashboard, and click "Add Listing". Fill in the property details and upload up to 3 photos. Your listing is submitted for moderation review and goes live once approved.
Do I pay anything to list?
No! Listing is completely free. There are no listing fees, monthly charges, or hidden costs. You only benefit when tenants unlock your contact.
How do I know when someone is interested?
You'll receive real-time notifications when a tenant unlocks your contact. You can also see all unlocks in your dashboard. Interested tenants will then reach out to you directly.

Safety & Security

How are landlords verified against scams?
During landlord registration, owners must submit their Zimbabwe National ID and property proof (Title Deed, Cession Agreement, or Council Offer Letter). Our AI document screener checks document legibility, hashes detect duplicate deeds across accounts, and Admin approves legitimate landlords before listings go live. Always inspect properties in person and follow our safety tips before paying deposits.
How do I report a fake listing?
Use the "Report" button on any listing page, or visit our Report page. We take all reports seriously and investigate within 24 hours.
