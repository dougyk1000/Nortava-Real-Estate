Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the property platform:

  • Account & Profile Data: Full name, email address, phone number, role (tenant or landlord), and age-eligibility confirmation (birth month and year checked during signup; we do not store full dates of birth unnecessarily).
  • Listing & Review Content: Property descriptions, pricing, neighborhood details, uploaded property photographs, and reviews submitted by signed-in users.
  • Support, Abuse & Copyright Notices: Messages submitted via our contact, report, or DMCA forms, including age confirmation (birth month/year) for guest submissions.
  • Payment & Unlock Records: Transaction reference, selected payment method, amount ($2.50), and unlock status.
  • Optional Analytics (Opt-In Only): Aggregated listing view counts only when you explicitly consent to optional analytics. We do not use session replay, heatmaps, keystroke logging, or third-party ad trackers.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Age Restrictions)

Nortava is not directed to children under 13, and we strictly prohibit anyone under 13 from creating an account or submitting personal information through any form (registration, contact, listing reports, or DMCA notices). Our server verifies birth month and year prior to processing or storing personal information; rejected under-age attempts are blocked without persisting personal information. In addition, account creation requires users to be at least 18 years old under our Terms of Service.

3. Third-Party Processors & Infrastructure

We self-host all fonts and icons locally and do not load third-party font CDNs or analytics SDKs. When enabled and configured by the platform operator, data is processed by:

  • Supabase Inc.: Authentication, PostgreSQL database hosting, and property image storage.
  • Local Payment Providers (EcoCash, OneMoney, Mukuru, InnBucks): Used solely to process tenant contact-unlock payments ($2.50 one-time fee) once merchant integrations are configured.
  • Application Hosting Provider: Receives standard HTTPS network metadata (such as IP address and request path) necessary to deliver web pages and API responses securely.

We share landlord contact details (name, phone, email) with a tenant only after a verified contact unlock for that listing. We never sell your personal information.

4. Email Communications & Unsubscribe

Nortava does not currently send promotional or marketing email campaigns. Essential transactional communications (such as email verification or password resets) are sent strictly as needed for account security. If optional marketing emails are ever introduced in the future, they will require explicit opt-in consent and include a one-click unsubscribe mechanism.

5. Your Rights & Consent Controls

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, as well as withdraw consent for non-essential browser storage or analytics at any time using Cookie Settings in the footer or on our Cookie Policy page.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain account and listing records while your account remains active and enforce Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, HTTPS transport security, and input sanitization. Upon verified deletion requests, personal data is removed within 30 days unless retention is required by law or payment recordkeeping obligations.

7. Contact Us

For privacy requests, data deletion, or questions, please contact us or review our DMCA Policy.
