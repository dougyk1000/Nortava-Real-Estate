2. How to Submit a Copyright Takedown Notice (Zimbabwe [Chapter 26:05] & 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3))

If you believe user-uploaded listing text or property photographs infringe your copyright under Zimbabwe's Copyright and Neighbouring Rights Act [Chapter 26:05] or international law, please submit a written notice containing all of the following elements:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • Identification of the allegedly infringing material on Nortava and its exact listing URL.
  • Your full legal name, postal mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury / statutory declaration, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

3. Counter-Notification & Repeat Infringer Policy

If your listing or image was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notification identifying the removed material, your contact details, a statement that removal resulted from mistake or misidentification, consent to jurisdiction, and your physical or electronic signature.

Repeat Infringer Policy: In accordance with Zimbabwean copyright law, the DMCA, and our Terms of Service, Nortava suspends or permanently terminates accounts of users who repeatedly submit infringing photographs or listing content.
