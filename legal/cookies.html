Cookie & Browser Storage Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Browser Storage Overview

Nortava avoids third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, and session replay tools. Instead, we use minimal browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) strictly as described below. In compliance with Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] and SI 155 of 2024, non-essential preference storage and optional analytics remain disabled until you explicitly opt in.

2. Storage Inventory

Key / Storage Category Purpose Consent Required?
sb-*-auth-token (localStorage) Essential Supabase authentication session token for signed-in users. No (Strictly Necessary)
nortava-user (localStorage) Essential Signed-in user summary for fast navigation rendering. No (Strictly Necessary)
nortava-consent (localStorage) Essential Stores your privacy and consent choices. No (Strictly Necessary)
nortava-chat-history-v2 (sessionStorage) Essential (Session) Keeps your current tab's AI property-assistant conversation until the tab closes. No (Session Functionality)
nortava-notifications (localStorage) Functional (Signed-in) Stores in-app dashboard notifications for signed-in accounts. No (Account Feature)
nortava-theme (localStorage) Preferences Remembers your light or dark theme selection across visits. Yes (Preferences Opt-In)
Listing View Telemetry (increment_views) Analytics Counts aggregated listing page views. No session replay or personal profiling. Yes (Analytics Opt-In)

3. Third-Party Cookies & External CDNs

All fonts (`Inter`) and icons (`RemixIcon`) are self-hosted inside our repository so third-party font CDNs do not receive your IP address on page load. We do not embed Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, PostHog, Hotjar, FullStory, Microsoft Clarity, or advertising cookies.

4. Withdrawing Consent

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clicking Manage Privacy & Cookie Settings above or Cookie Settings in any page footer. Turning off Preferences removes `nortava-theme` from browser storage immediately.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about browser storage or privacy, please contact us.
